runik

(ˈrü-nik)

a cross platform desktop app for generating and managing custom e-reader dictionaries.

Download

Enhance your reading experience!

Stay immersed in your story with in-text definitions for fictional items, characters, and locations.

How it works

  • Find a wiki for the book or series you are reading and provide runik the URL.
  • Runik processes each wiki page, compiling them into a dictionary unique to your book.
    • 
              
    
            
    
            
  • Runik converts the dictionary to the appropriate format and installs it onto your e-reader device.
